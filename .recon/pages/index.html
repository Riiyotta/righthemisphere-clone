faq's

01
Who we work with
Brands that believe in the intangible value of design. Teams that want a development partner to get their visions built without compromises.
02
What is our tech stack
We build on Webflow, Framer, Wix, Shopify and Wordpress. If required, we write custom code for automations and added functionalities.
03
Do we migrate websites
Yes. A large chunk of our work involves frictionless migration of websites from legacy systems to new ones. We work closely with your old team to ensure you regain control over your website.
04
Do we offer website maintenance
Yes. Every site we build comes with 2 months of free maintenance post-handover. Beyond that we have maintenance retainers which is way more economical than keeping an in-house tech team on payroll.
05
Do we offer a free UI and UX audit
Yes. If you are unsure about your website being user-friendly, fill out the contact form and we will sit with you to give you honest and research-backed feedback.
